    Questions we get often

    What does delivery cost?

    Delivery is ₹29 on orders below ₹299 and free from ₹299 upward. The minimum order value is ₹99.

    How do delivery slots work?

    At checkout you choose a two-hour window for today or tomorrow. Slots for today close two hours before they begin.

    What if something is not fresh?

    Report it within 24 hours of delivery with a photo. You choose a refund to your original payment method or a replacement in your next slot.

    How are loose items weighed?

    Packs are weighed at packing and checked again before sealing. You pay for the pack weight shown on the label.

    What do the Organic and In season tags mean?

    Organic marks produce sold from our separate organic shelf, with certification details on the pack. In season marks produce that is at its natural peak this month.

    Which payment methods can I use?

    UPI, cards and net banking on a secure payment page, or cash on delivery.
